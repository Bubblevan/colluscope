# Independent Scientific Critic — A/B/C Paper-Worthiness Review

**Reviewer mode:** separate-context, blind to prior recommendation documents and the candidate ranking at the time of review

**Access:** sanitized reports and aggregates only; no raw private messages, no raw response bytes

**Actions:** no model/API calls, no tests, no edits

**Scope limitation:** desk review of reported evidence, not an independent raw-data replay.

## Verdicts

### A. B5 evidence-view instability and all-present failure — INCONCLUSIVE

The finite observations are supported: the Branch A report records 20/24 Public-only/Privileged prediction changes with a fixed reference, and the subsequent campaign found 19/24 changes for that contrast. Historical/current exact-request responses also disagreed in 19/48 pairs despite identical request bytes and matching reported fingerprints. These facts do not identify what caused the view comparisons.

The strongest counterexample is the combination of bundled input changes and the exact deterministic rule baseline. The synthetic B5 control returned `present` for all 40 balanced rows (20/40 exact), whereas the deterministic rule matched all 40. The arithmetic form's gain is confounded with extra content and length; the reversed form changes both prose and enum order. A broad context-access contribution overlaps Arike et al. A narrower exact-request repeatability result could be useful, but the current evidence does not establish an LLM advantage or paper-level mechanism.

### B. Shared reward, communication content, and Agent choice — INCONCLUSIVE

The reported finite result is 9/15 target executions for private reward plus chat versus 0/15 in the matched no-chat arm. This is not a causal message-content result. Shared-target knowledge and an added decision turn remain confounds; M3 showed Proposal/Masked ballot agreement in 8/8, and the same 3/8 ballots changed under both Neutral and No-delivery, with only one settled allocation change.

Communication/action studies are already present in Colosseum, double-auction work, long-horizon peer interventions, and recent competing-incentive consensus research. A new result would need to separate information received from shared incentives and stage effects. Its potential is higher than C but its current evidence is not confirmatory.

### C. Evidence sufficiency and identifiability — SUPPORTED for the frozen synthetic fixture only

The reported 12 matched pairs have identical Public-only prepared projections; 8/12 pairs differ on at least one determinate privileged sequence element, while only 2/12 differ on a complete sequence. This supports a finite witness for the fixture, not a general impossibility result or LLM-monitor capability.

The strongest counterexample to making C the paper's primary contribution is that identical public views and different privileged events were construction targets, and the exact sequence parser is deterministic. It is valuable as a claim-boundary/control suite but weak as a standalone discovery.

## Ranking and recommendation

For near-term cost and boundedness, the reviewer ranked C first, B second, and A third. The reviewer specifically warned that C alone may amount to confirming its own construction and is not enough for a strong paper. It judged structured public welfare mechanically computable and recommended code as the baseline.

Select A for the next diagnostic because it directly addresses the verified real M4 anomaly and the all-present failure. A remains a high-risk paper contribution: if the controlled factors do not identify a useful failure mode, or if no validated LLM role remains beyond deterministic calculation, stop rather than inflate the result.
