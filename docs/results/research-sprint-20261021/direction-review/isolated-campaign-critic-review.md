# Isolated Adversarial Review — M4 B5 Context-Sensitivity Campaign

**Review date:** 2026-10-21 (Asia/Shanghai)  
**Scope:** Read-only review of the registered campaign before dispatch. No model/API calls and no source edits by the reviewer.  
**Verdict:** `INCONCLUSIVE` on mechanism; no fatal integrity defect found in the frozen design.

## What the campaign can test

A1 is the most direct test of the real historical anomaly: in 24 fixed M4 contexts, compare Public-only, Privileged-original, and Privileged-neutral B5 labels. A positive result can support a finite descriptive statement about response differences among those views. It cannot establish message-semantic causality, natural-agent intent, monitor improvement, generalization, or reproduce the earlier provider behavior because `deepseek-flash` is a mutable serving alias and the preregistration does not require current Public-only/original predictions to match historical labels.

A2 is a separate, supportive diagnostic of the earlier 40/40 `present` synthetic output. It varies request construction, adds six unranked allocation totals, and reverses label order. The corrected arithmetic aid contains no precomputed optimum, realized welfare, regret, reference label, or answer field. Given the public outcome and raw totals, the answer remains mechanically computable; deterministic replay is the primary baseline. A2 therefore tests finite-fixture request-form sensitivity, not added LLM capability.

## Integrity checks and limitations

- Registered schedule and public manifest contain 232 matching assignment rows; A1 has 24 assigned cases and 23 unique Public-only/neutral ordered request profiles. The duplicate cases remain in the denominator and are covered by the registered duplicate-profile sensitivity.
- No evidence of hidden reference leakage was found in the corrected A2 request construction. Raw request bodies are locally protected and omitted from the public manifest, so the independent reviewer could verify hashes and construction code but not inspect every final body byte from the public packet.
- The Public-only/neutral contrast bundles event presence, visibility/provenance, completeness, ledger, serialization, and token length. Neutral/original changes wording, salience, and length with the text. No semantic interpretation is warranted.
- Arike et al. already study information access and Extract-and-Evaluate. This campaign does not evaluate EaE, sabotage detection, or a mitigation; any contribution is complementary and narrow: fixed-case response sensitivity to evidence-view packaging in a mechanically scoreable multi-agent task.
- If neither preregistered A1 directional pattern appears, report `INCONCLUSIVE`. Keep A1 primary and A2 secondary; do not elevate the synthetic diagnostic into a general monitor result.

## Decision

The campaign is suitable to proceed as the exact, bounded, user-approved response-sensitivity campaign after the remaining dispatch gates pass. Its scientific value is a direct diagnosis of the B5 anomaly, not an expected positive result. The 160-call A2 block is justified only as a supporting test of the explicit all-present failure mechanisms; it is not the headline contribution.
