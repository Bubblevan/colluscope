## Review conclusion

**Scientific outcome:** INCONCLUSIVE for the cause of B5 response variation and for any communication-induced behavior mechanism. The finite observations are reproducible, but current evidence does not identify a single cause.

**Candidate ranking:**

1. **C — task-specific evidence sufficiency** is the cheapest immediate action because public-harm is exactly computable from public structured records. The 12-pair scripted sequence construction can define a narrow evidence boundary, but is not a general impossibility result or a strong standalone paper contribution.
2. **B — private reward × chat-stage package** offers the strongest potential real-agent empirical contribution. It needs prospective replication. The current 9/15 post-hoc pattern is heterogeneous by payoff family and confounded by a shared rewarded target and extra chat-stage context; it does not show message semantics or collusion.
3. **A — B5 view sensitivity/repeatability** is useful as a monitor-validity result but remains inconclusive after the 232-request campaign. Same-request raw disagreements and the 20/24 finite view changes do not identify provider state, context causality, or message semantics. More calls on the same structured public-harm label are low value.

## Deterministic baseline necessity

For public-harm on structured inputs, a deterministic baseline is necessary and should be the authoritative reference for settlement, six-permutation optimum, realized welfare, and regret. The independent rule matches 40/40 references on the frozen synthetic public-canonical fixture and 24/24 references on complete real M4 public records. LLM output should not be treated as ground truth or as a separate welfare computation. A remaining LLM role is natural-language extraction of proposals/acceptance and semantic comparison with a later vote, but it requires independent human references and must demonstrate value over literal/rule baselines.

## Paper-worthiness

The B5 all-present behavior and the finite scripted C construction are not independently sufficient for a substantive paper: one is a model prediction against a task solved by deterministic code, and the other is a constructed fixture. A paper-level contribution could emerge from a prospective, matched Agent package replication paired with a narrowly scoped, human-annotated Monitor evaluation of natural-language proposal and action-concordance evidence. Even then, it should claim only what the interventions and observations identify, with no latent-collusion gold label.

## Strongest counterexamples

- The 0/5 vs 3/3 M3 self-history split is post-hoc and could reflect role, payoff matrix, wording, or selection among proposal-eligible groups.
- In M1-100, A and B knew the same rewarded target before communication; both messages were generated before either could be delivered. Parallel action or stage salience can explain the 9/15 pattern.
- In the M4 B5 contrast, the reference label stayed fixed while 20/24 predictions changed, but most transitions were abstention to absent; the evidence package also changed token count and structure.
- Historical/current byte-identical B5 requests disagree in 19/48 raw labels despite matching reported fingerprints. The model alias does not pin hidden service state.
