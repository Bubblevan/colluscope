## Mechanism-language audit

The manuscript distinguishes: (a) **assignment** in T; (b) **actual action** in B, including send/delivery and ballot; (c) **permitted monitor evidence** in E; (d) **deterministic calculations** such as allocation settlement, welfare and regret; (e) **model predictions** in M2; (f) **counterfactual model responses** in M3; and (g) **latent intent**, which remains unknown. No task output is called a collusion gold label. “Proposal,” “match,” “target execution,” “harm/regret,” “treatment,” and “delivery” refer only to their operational definitions in the frozen audit protocol.

The three M3 changed ballots are not described as collusion, proof of a communication effect, or a general causal result. The subgroup is visibly labeled post hoc exploratory. M2's perfect cell is explicitly described as answer-exposed. The results section does not use fixed-peer replays as observed episodes. The paper's causal language is limited to assignment/design descriptions and statements that current data do not identify a causal effect.
